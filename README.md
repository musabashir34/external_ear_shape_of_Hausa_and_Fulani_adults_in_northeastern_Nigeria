# Ear shape of Hausa and Fulani adults: reproducible article

This repository holds the raw landmark data and the R Markdown source of the article

> *Sexual dimorphism, ethnic variation and bilateral asymmetry in external ear shape of Hausa and Fulani adults in northeastern Nigeria: a geometric morphometric study*.

Knitting `manuscript.Rmd` starts from the raw tpsDig2 files and produces:

- the article itself, as `manuscript.docx`: every number in the text, every table, the figures and the reference list are generated by the code;
- the six figures, in `output/figures/` as PDF (vector), TIFF (600 dpi, for submission) and PNG;
- the supplementary tables (Tables S1–S10), in `output/` as `Additional_file_1.docx` (formatted, with notes), `Additional_file_1.xlsx` and CSV files.

### Identifiers

`FFRE_004` = **F**emale, **F**ulani, **R**ight **E**ar, participant 004. Groups: FF Fulani female, FH Hausa female,
MF Fulani male, MH Hausa male. Specimen IDs are taken from the image names in the tpsDig2 files (`FFRE 004.jpg` →
`FFRE_004`). Two right-ear images of Hausa males were mislabelled at digitization and have been corrected in
`right_ears_raw.TPS` (MHRE 018 and MHRE 058); any further corrections can be listed in `data/raw/image_renames.csv`.

## Reproducing the article

Requirements: R ≥ 4.3 with `geomorph` (4.1.1 used), `RRPP`, `Morpho`, `MASS`, `abind`, `flextable`, `officer`,
`openxlsx`, `rmarkdown` and `knitr`.

```r
install.packages(c("geomorph", "RRPP", "Morpho", "abind", "flextable", "officer", "openxlsx", "rmarkdown"))
rmarkdown::render("manuscript.Rmd")
```

With 9999 permutations for every test, the first run takes about 10–15 minutes. The analysis chunk is cached, so later
runs that change only the text take seconds. For a quick check, use fewer permutations:

```r
rmarkdown::render("manuscript.Rmd", params = list(iter = 99))
```

The article shows each figure, with its caption and alternative text, after the paragraph that first cites it, and
follows the author guidelines of *Anthropological Review* (APA 7th edition references, American spelling, line numbers,
1.5 line spacing). By default the knit produces the anonymised manuscript for double-blind review: no title page, and
identifying details withheld. The non-anonymised manuscript, with the title page and full declarations, must be asked for:

```r
rmarkdown::render("manuscript.Rmd", params = list(blind = FALSE), output_file = "manuscript_non-anonymised.docx")
```

Figure captions can instead be collected at the end, without the images, with `params = list(embed_figures = FALSE)`.

The analysis stops with an explanation if the raw data are inconsistent, for example:

- an ear whose curve cannot be matched to its landmarks;
- a duplicated specimen ID;
- a participant without both ears.

## Author details and anonymity

The repository contains no author names. Names, affiliations, approval numbers and funding are read from
`private/author_details.R`, which is excluded from Git; copy `author_details_TEMPLATE.R` there and fill it in before knitting the
non-anonymised manuscript (`blind = FALSE`). Without that file, even that version shows placeholders, so the repository can be shared for
double-blind review. Knitted Word files (`manuscript*.docx`) are also excluded from Git.
